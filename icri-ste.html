ABOUT ICRI-STE

Connecting science, technology, engineering and innovation

ICRI-STE describes itself as a global network of scientists, technologists and engineers focused on multidisciplinary collaboration and research. Its stated mission emphasizes collaboration across applied science, artificial intelligence and data science.

The organization also highlights research and development areas including intelligent computational modeling, scientific machine learning, biomedical systems, biochemical systems, STEM exploration and intelligent innovation in AI.
