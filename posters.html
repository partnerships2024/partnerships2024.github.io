Recommended Poster Record

Each accepted poster may be presented using the following research information.

Research Title

Concise title of the scientific contribution, project, reproduction study, or research prototype.

Researchers

Author names, ECR level, research role, and affiliations where appropriate.

Research Area

AI, genomics, cancer research, biomedical systems, medical imaging, computational healthcare, bioinformatics, or another relevant theme.

CHI LAB RESEARCH

Suggested Poster Themes

Posters may highlight research, reproduction studies, computational experiments, AI-driven prototypes, and interdisciplinary healthcare research.

Computational Oncology

Lung cancer, pancreatic cancer, precision oncology, cancer genomics, biomarkers, and computational cancer research.

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, deep learning, transformers, explainable AI, trustworthy AI, and AI-driven research systems.

Biomedical Intelligence

Medical imaging, bioinformatics, systems biology, multi-omics analysis, biomedical control systems, and intelligent healthcare.

Reproducible Research

Research reproduction, open science, computational workflows, benchmarking, validation, and transparent scientific experimentation.

AI Agents & Virtual Labs

Agentic AI, human-in-the-loop research, AI-assisted scientific reasoning, privacy-preserving virtual labs, and computational research automation.

Early Career Research

ECR research projects, thesis work, research reproductions, emerging ideas, and interdisciplinary scientific collaborations.
