SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH

From Research Questions to Reproducible Implementation

The CHI Lab encourages Early Career Researchers to explore published research, reproduce established methods, critically analyse results, and develop new computational approaches in healthcare and biomedical research.

The resources below are provided as examples of high-quality research communication and implementation. They may serve as references for preparing research papers, scientific posters, presentations and reproducible computational projects.

Johns Hopkins University

MICCAI 2025
Research & Implementation Resource

Learning to Segment Any Tumor from Human-Annotated Images

Johns Hopkins University · MICCAI 2025

This featured research resource demonstrates an example of advanced artificial intelligence and medical image analysis research focused on tumour segmentation. It provides a useful reference for CHI Lab Scientists and Early Career Researchers interested in medical imaging, computational oncology, reproducible AI research and scientific communication.

Researchers are encouraged to study the publication, supplementary poster, institutional research coverage and presentation materials to understand how a computational healthcare research project can be developed, communicated and presented at an international scientific venue.
