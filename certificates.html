Official Verification Portal

Certificate Verification

Verify certificates issued by the Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab under the International Council for Research & Innovation in Science, Technology & Engineering (ICRI-STE).

Verify a Certificate

Enter the unique Certificate ID exactly as printed on the certificate.

Certificate IDs are case-insensitive. Example IDs are included for testing.

How Verification Works

Use the portal to confirm whether a certificate record exists in the official verification database.

01

Enter Certificate ID

Type the unique identification number shown on the issued certificate.

02

Verify Record

The portal checks the certificate ID against the records configured for this website.

03

Review Details

For a valid record, the portal displays the certificate holder, programme, issue date and status.

Important: A certificate should be considered verified only when its Certificate ID and displayed details correspond to the original certificate. If a certificate cannot be verified, please contact CHI Lab / ICRI-STE through the official contact channel.
