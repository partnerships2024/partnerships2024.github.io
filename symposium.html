PURPOSE

Bringing CHI Lab research together

The symposium is designed as a shared platform for CHI Lab scientists and Early Career Researchers to communicate their research, demonstrate emerging computational approaches and engage with a broader scientific community.

Contributions may include original research, computational studies, methodological developments, research prototypes, interdisciplinary work and scientific posters.
