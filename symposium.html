Scientists & Early Career Researchers Symposium

Programme Participation

ABOUT THE SYMPOSIUM

Research. Present. Collaborate. Reproduce.

The symposium is designed to provide CHI Lab scientists and ECRs with a structured platform to communicate ongoing research, demonstrate computational workflows, receive scientific feedback and identify opportunities for collaboration.

OrganiserComputational Healthcare Intelligence Lab (CHI Lab)
HostInternational Council for Research & Innovation in STE (ICRI-STE)
AudienceScientists, ECRs, research collaborators & invited guests
FormatScientific presentations • Research demonstrations • Discussion

PROPOSED PROGRAMME

Symposium structure

01

Opening & CHI Lab Vision

Welcome, objectives and the role of computational healthcare intelligence.

02

Scientists' Research Presentations

Selected research talks covering AI/ML, systems biology, genomics and healthcare intelligence.

03

ECR Research Presentations

Early Career Researchers present projects, experiments, results and reproducible workflows.

04

Research Demonstrations

AI agents, federated learning, computational pipelines, medical imaging and virtual-lab prototypes.

05

Scientific Discussion & Collaboration

Feedback, open questions, collaboration opportunities and next research milestones.
